Tree view of the volumes

HFS volumes are shown with white icons. They may be HFV or DSK volume files (), CD's () or floppy disks (). Windows volumes are shown with yellow icons; hard disks (), floppies (), network drives (), CD's () or RAM disks ().

The tree view is updated automatically when you insert or remove a CD, but only if you have auto-insert notification enabled. Switching floppies always requires you to hit the Refresh command (F5 in keyboard). You can, if you want, modify your HFVExplorer.ini file located in the Windows home directory,  to make HFVExplorer try to detect Mac floppies when the floppy icon is clicked:

      [Setup]
      DisableAutoFloppy=0

This feature is disabled by default, due to some unresolved (rare) crash reports. Use at your own risk.

Clicking an volume file displays its contents. You can also use (keyboard) arrow keys to move in the tree, just like in Windows Explorer.

You can press "Tabulator" key (keyboard) to switch between volume tree and volume contents.
 

The uppermost entry in the tree, "Configurations", is not yet implemented. (I don't even remember any more what was the bright idea.)
